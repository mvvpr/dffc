FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a gym?

No. Your plan is built around the equipment you have: a full gym, a few dumbbells or just your bodyweight.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Your programme starts where you are, with clear instructions and video feedback so you learn the right technique.

How does online coaching work?

You train on your own schedule following your plan, then check in weekly so Diana can review your progress and adjust.

Is the consult call really free?

Yes. It's a no-pressure conversation to see whether coaching is the right fit for you.

Does Diana coach in Portuguese?

Yes. Diana coaches clients in both English and Portuguese.
